About CINEC Campus

Colombo International Nautical & Engineering College (CINEC), established in 1990, is Sri Lanka's pioneer in Merchant Marine Maritime Education and stands as one of the largest private higher education institutions in the country.

Located in the suburbs of Malabe, CINEC has a proud history spanning over three decades. Over the years, it has transformed from a top maritime academy in South Asia into a highly diversified, fast-growing academic hub catering to over 22,000 student registrations annually across certificate, diploma, undergraduate, and postgraduate programs.

CINEC offers premium local and globally recognized university degrees in fields such as Maritime Science, Engineering & Technology, Information Technology, Computer Science, Management, Health Sciences, Logistics, Aviation, and Education in partnership with renowned global universities in the UK, Australia, USA, and China.

With fully functional branches in Nugegoda, Trincomalee, and Jaffna, the campus remains deeply committed to preparing world-class career professionals built for global industry tracks.
